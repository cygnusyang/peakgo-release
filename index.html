PeakGo（峰行）隐私政策

适用于 Android 应用 top.cygnusyang.peakgo · 生效日期：2026-11-15

一句话：PeakGo 不收集你的任何数据。 应用不申请网络权限，所有读数、照片和设置都只留在你自己的设备上。

1. 我们不收集、不上传任何数据

PeakGo 的 Android 清单里没有 INTERNET 权限。 这不是一句承诺，而是系统层面的限制：应用没有联网能力，也就无法把数据发到任何地方。 我们没有服务器、没有账号体系、没有统计 SDK、没有广告 SDK。

2. 权限与用途

  • 位置（精确 / 大致）：用于计算海拔、显示经纬度、计算真北所需的磁偏角， 以及水印相机里要写进照片的坐标。位置数据在设备内实时计算，不保存历史轨迹，不上传。
  • 相机：仅在你打开「水印相机」时申请，用于取景拍照。 照片由系统相册保存到你的设备，应用不读取相册里的其他照片。

两项权限都在你实际使用对应功能时申请；拒绝位置权限时，其余功能（指南针、水平仪、 气压趋势）照常可用，只是海拔与坐标显示为不可用。

3. 保存在你设备上的内容

  • 你的偏好设置（单位、主题、语言）与海拔校准参数，存放在应用私有存储中。
  • 你拍摄的照片，由系统相册（MediaStore）保存到你的设备。
  • 以上内容都不会离开设备。卸载应用即清除应用私有存储中的全部数据。

4. 第三方共享

没有。应用内不集成任何第三方 SDK（分析、崩溃上报、广告、推送均无）， 也不存在「与合作方共享」这种条款——因为没有可共享的通道。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用是户外读数工具，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息（包括儿童）。

6. 政策变更

如果将来引入任何需要联网的功能，本页会在功能上线前更新，并在应用内说明。 届时收集范围、用途与关闭方式会逐项写清楚。

7. 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，或想确认某个行为，请写信到 ruohuyang@gmail.com。

PeakGo Privacy Policy

For the Android app top.cygnusyang.peakgo · Effective 15 November 2026

In one sentence: PeakGo collects nothing. The app does not request the network permission, so every reading, photo and setting stays on your device.

1. No collection, no upload

PeakGo's Android manifest has no INTERNET permission. That is not a promise, it is an operating-system limit: the app cannot reach a network, so it cannot send data anywhere. There is no server, no account system, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

2. Permissions and why they exist

  • Location (precise / approximate) — used to compute altitude, show coordinates, derive the magnetic declination needed for true north, and stamp coordinates on watermarked photos. Location is processed on the device in real time; no location history is stored or uploaded.
  • Camera — requested only when you open the watermark camera, for the live preview and shutter. Photos are saved to your device by the system gallery; the app does not read other photos.

Both permissions are requested at the moment you use the matching feature. If you deny location, the compass, level and pressure trend keep working; altitude and coordinates are simply shown as unavailable.

3. What is stored on your device

  • Your preferences (units, theme, language) and altitude calibration values, in the app's private storage.
  • Photos you take, saved to your device through the system gallery (MediaStore).
  • None of it leaves the device. Uninstalling the app clears all of its private data.

4. Third parties

None. No third-party SDKs are bundled — no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no push. There is no "sharing with partners" clause, because there is no channel to share through.

5. Children

This is an outdoor reading tool, not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

6. Changes

If a future version adds anything that needs a network connection, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the app will say so. What is collected, why, and how to turn it off will be spelled out item by item.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about any specific behaviour, go to ruohuyang@gmail.com.
